# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the AgentCheck community a respectful, welcoming, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and participants to interact professionally and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate in discussions.
* Providing constructive feedback.
* Respecting different opinions, experiences, and technical approaches.
* Focusing discussions on ideas, code, and project goals rather than individuals.
* Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
* Helping maintain a productive and collaborative open-source environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Insulting, inflammatory, or deliberately disruptive comments.
* Trolling or repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Threats or inappropriate conduct of any kind.
* Any behavior that would reasonably make participation in the project unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all AgentCheck community spaces, including:

* GitHub issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Code reviews
* Project-related communication
* Other public spaces where someone is representing the AgentCheck project

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or close comments, commits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant from the project.

Enforcement decisions should be made fairly and consistently.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the AgentCheck maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Project maintainers are expected to respect the privacy and safety of anyone reporting an incident.
